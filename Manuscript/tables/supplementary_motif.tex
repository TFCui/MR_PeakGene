\footnotesize
Motif enrichment was tested for peaks selected by each method
(within-gene BH-adjusted \(\q<0.1\)) against a background matched on GC content and accessibility
(Signac, JASPAR2020 vertebrate CORE motifs). The table lists MR-specific motifs:
p.adj \(<0.01\) and fold enrichment \(>1\) in at least one of IVW, MR-Egger,
and SMR (bold), with no significant enrichment (p.adj \(\ge0.05\)) in WNN or Pseudobulk,
and a clear literature-based interpretation. p.adj is the adjusted motif-enrichment
p-value reported by Signac for each method.
Reference (PMID) lists the PubMed identifiers of the papers on which each interpretation
is based. For each motif, the literature on the corresponding transcription factor(s) was
searched in PubMed for functions in the same or a related immune cell type, or in
immune-mediated disease, and every PMID was checked against its PubMed record
(title, year, and abstract).
Pseudobulk, pseudobulk correlation; WNN, WNN metacell correlation;
CCT, Cauchy combination of SMR, IVW, and MR-Egger; Bn, naive B cells;
CD4/CD8, naive CD4/CD8 T cells. Rows are ordered as in the source table.
