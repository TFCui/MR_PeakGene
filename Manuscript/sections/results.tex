\subsection{A common analysis set enables comparison of six linking methods}
\label{res:analysis-set}
We used cell-type-specific CIMA caQTL and eQTL summary statistics to investigate
accessibility-to-expression links in naive B cells (Bn), naive CD4 T cells (CD4),
and naive CD8 T cells (CD8). The six methods were GLS-IVW, GLS-MR Egger, SMR,
an equal-weight Cauchy combination of these three MR tests (CCT), WNN metacell
Spearman correlation, and donor-level pseudobulk Spearman correlation
(Fig~\ref{fig:overview}A).




\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth,height=0.7\textheight,keepaspectratio]{Figure_1.pdf}
\caption{The complete analysis framework and the results of  CD4 cells.
\textbf{A} MR methods (IVW, MR Egger, SMR) use cell-type specific caQTL/eQTL summary statistics; Marginal multiomics uses external PBMC metacells, and marginal pseudobulk correlations use CIMA individuals. Both two marginal correlation are measured by the Spearman correlation.
\textbf{B} UpSet plot of significant links in CD4 cells (p-adjust < 0.1, BH)
\textbf{C} Effect directions among the 1,259 CD4 links significant in all six
methods. Slices compare the sign of the IVW estimate with the signs of the
marginal correlations: concordant with both marginal methods (light gray), discordant with Marginal Multiomics only (blue), discordant with Marginal Pseudobulk only (green), and discordant with both (brown). Bn and CD8 comparisons appear in \nameref{S3_Fig}.}
\label{fig:overview}
\end{figure}

\label{res:references}

PCHiC reference datasets.

\subsection{MR-only regions show exploratory disease-heritability enrichment}
\label{res:sldsc}
The disease-heritability summary compares the combined MR-only peak
annotation with the all-peaks annotation across nine GWAS
(Fig~\ref{fig:disease}A). 

The selected main-text examples are RGL2 in Bn and ERBB3, POLR2E, and UBAC2
in CD4 (Fig~\ref{fig:disease}B).


\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[page=1,width=\linewidth]{Figure_4.pdf}

\textbf{A} Heritability enrichment of the combined MR-only and
all-candidate-peak S-LDSC annotations for each GWAS trait, shown on a common
y axis; asterisks mark the annotated enrichment results (p value < 0.1).
\textbf{B}  Tracks show the gene, MR instruments (dark-blue ticks), GWAS variants within the peak (purple diamonds), the peak (gold bar), and one link
