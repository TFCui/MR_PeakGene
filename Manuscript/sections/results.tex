\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{A common analysis set enables comparison of six linking methods}
\label{res:analysis-set}
We used cell-type-specific CIMA caQTL and eQTL summary statistics to investigate
accessibility-to-expression links in naive B cells (Bn), naive CD4$^+$ T cells (CD4 T cells),
and naive CD8$^+$ T cells (CD8 T cells). The six methods were GLS-IVW, GLS-MR Egger, SMR,
an equal-weight Cauchy combination of these three MR tests (CCT), WNN metacell
Spearman correlation, and donor-level pseudobulk Spearman correlation
(Fig~\ref{fig:overview}A).

\caption{The complete analysis framework and the results of CD4 T cells.
\textbf{A} MR methods (IVW, MR Egger, SMR) use cell-type specific caQTL/eQTL summary statistics; Marginal multiomics uses external PBMC metacells, and marginal pseudobulk correlations use CIMA individuals. Both two marginal correlation are measured by the Spearman correlation.
\textbf{B} UpSet plot of significant links in CD4 T cells (p-adjust < 0.1, BH)
\textbf{C} Effect directions among the 1,259 CD4 T cell links significant in all six
methods. Slices compare the sign of the IVW estimate with the signs of the
marginal correlations: concordant with both marginal methods (light gray), discordant with Marginal Multiomics only (blue), discordant with Marginal Pseudobulk only (green), and discordant with both (brown). Bn and CD8 T cell comparisons appear in \nameref{S3_Fig}.}

\subsection{Selected links are enriched for regulatory-reference support}
\label{res:references}
We evaluated links against EnhancerAtlas, FANTOM5, 4DGenome, GTEx, HiChIP, and
PCHiC reference datasets.

\subsection{GO summaries characterize biological processes and molecular functions}
\label{res:function}
The updated GO summary displays Biological Process and Molecular Function
terms (Fig~\ref{fig:go}). 

\begin{figure}[htbp]

\subsection{MR-only regions show exploratory disease-heritability enrichment}
\label{res:sldsc}
The disease-heritability summary compares the combined MR-only peak
annotation with the all-peaks annotation across seven GWAS
(Fig~\ref{fig:disease}A). 

The selected main-text examples are RGL2 in Bn, ERBB3, POLR2E, and UBAC2
in CD4 T cells, and GNAS in CD8 T cells (Fig~\ref{fig:disease}B).
